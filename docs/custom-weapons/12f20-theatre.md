# CW-12f20 — opt-in ordinary theatre playback

This source contract describes default-off custom archive viewing.
Local ordinary-viewing qualification and limits are recorded in the
[evidence ledger](evidence/CW-12f20.md).

`PROJECT_PRIME_CUSTOM_WEAPON_PLAYBACK=1` enables the custom branch of ordinary
`DemoPlayback.Join`. The request captures an absolute approved local store root
from configuration/default user storage and prepares exact historical package
resources on a worker. No install, download, fallback or gameplay grant occurs.
Native constructors and admission remain separate and unchanged.

Opening is generation-fenced. A serialized preparation gate bounds concurrent
work; cancellation and supersession dispose stale prepared ownership. The
request host exposes the admitted archive baseline while a Scene has not yet
been created. An existing player/lab must be closed before a custom request can
replace it. Stop retires pending preparation and its retained resources.

The foreground Scene thread adopts the prepared owner through the bounded
`PassiveReplayPlayer` foundation. Custom updates use at most120 fixed steps and
a5ms seek budget; native update timing remains unchanged. Worker seeks restore
fresh Scenes with exact baseline checks and independently retained historical
resources. Scene replacement and disposal stay on the original Scene thread.

Custom subject selection and camera state are private to playback. They do not
mutate native global spectator ownership. The selected subject is revalidated
against recorded lifecycle/eligibility; exact package settled optics are projected
without claiming recorded owner transitions. Playback is silent and suppresses
the retail visor/ammo HUD; replay presentation callbacks remain separate.

Ordinary custom Studio windows are viewer-only. Interactive selection saving,
virtual clip creation, practice takeover, video export and detached export intake
remain refused before output or private export Scene/GL creation. Historical
format7 `ReplayArchive.Extract` remains a storage operation; it is not interactive
clip/export admission, and its existing historical tests remain intact.

This slice does not enable killcam, server capture, instant clips or owner/audio
sidecar fidelity. Full CW-12, bots and every hunter/form remain open. Android is
deferred; CI, WAN, physical audio and performance budgets are unqualified.
