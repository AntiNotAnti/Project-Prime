# CW-04f authority contact integration

`Scene.EnableDeveloperCustomCombat(true, trustedSourceAdmission)` is an explicit,
internal developer seam for authoritative Battle fixtures. There is no packet,
launcher option or automatic room activation. The supplied source oracle is trusted
engine code, not a client certificate. Authenticated input capture, readiness,
transport and replay integration remain required before enabling custom matches.

The scene owns eight private projectile pools, at most 32 retained owner lives and
128 canonical death records. Each life owns its ordered resource journal and bounded
128-entry payment store. Match/epoch/actor/occupant replacement and spectating retire
its pools, journals and receipts. Respawn retires the old input journal while retaining
admitted old-life flights and outcomes; a new life receives its separate initial
inventory once. Exhausted retention refuses new admission rather than evicting live
entries. The adapter is absent in ordinary scenes and rejects unsupported rule modes.

Emission rechecks the trusted source certificate, full owner, capacity and mandatory
custom trade decision. The private equipment supplies canonical collision and head
classification under the explicit custom rewind scope. A definite no-spawn refunds
only the fire debit; uncertain execution quarantines the journal. Source-time pose,
modifiers and deterministic spread drive emission. No native weapon alias or ShotKey
is fabricated.

A physical contact first reserves its single component in the life-owned payment
store. Only that reservation can grant one entry into canonical TakeDamage. A direct,
forged, stale, cross-scene or reentrant custom damage call is refused. Before/after
body and turret health establish actual removal; protected zero contacts still consume
the component. Accepted contacts retain a typed immutable outcome and actual firing
life for generic assist attribution. Native weapon/headshot statistics, native claim
history and native replay producers do not receive fabricated custom records.

Positive custom removal allocates the shared victim damage-event sequence and clears
native latest-event attribution. It leaves a hole in native history. Ancient native
history entries are pruned before ushort identity collision or half-range ambiguity;
a lifecycle-validated canonical snapshot closes such holes only after retained native
events have been read. A custom receipt alone cannot advance the native replay frontier.

Custom trade checks run independently of optional native claim/proof policy. Canonical
deaths retain their declared launch world for the full owner life; equal integer world
frames may trade. Old-life flights require retained death proof. If a later earlier
kill invalidates an emitted flight, its first physical contact becomes a terminal
zero-effect TradeDenied result. It retains the emitted shot's ammo debit and cannot
revive through a different target, channel or later death undo. This slice does not
claim to replace the existing native arbitration algorithm or establish authenticated
source timing.

The real scene fixture invokes actual custom Spawn, Process, collision and TakeDamage,
actual native damage and respawn, and canonical snapshot/native replay paths. Its
source certificates are explicitly seeded. Halfturret channel testing invokes native
ResolveCollision under controlled placement, and pool pressure is synthetic. Lifecycle validation also covers rule/stream changes,
scene cleanup and the 32-life retention ceiling. Network
loss/reorder, host/remote source fairness, reconnect, reliable outcome delivery and
physical device/render gates remain unvalidated. CW-04 remains in progress.
