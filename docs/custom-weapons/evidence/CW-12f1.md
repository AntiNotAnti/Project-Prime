uses the historical literal CWPK/CWM1 reference. Tests reject every truncation,
trailing/oversized data, unsupported schema/capabilities, zero identities,
digest/length/runtime inconsistencies and a fully consistent multipackage
envelope. Defensive copies preserve the retained value.

Historical preparation opens actual uninstalled cached bytes, compares every
recorded identity, decodes exact four-cue PCM and model resources, and retains
independent ownership without filesystem access under a held store gate.
Prune skips active owners and succeeds after the last closes. Missing history,
a different newer metadata revision and preserved deliberately corrupt bytes
produce the fixed exact-revision unavailable diagnostic. Missing stores are
not created. Cancellation completes while the real gate remains owned; partial
binding acquires the actual first sorted package before the missing next entry
and releases that earlier lease. These checks do not construct a passive replay
scene or prove a seekable custom archive.
