# CW-05t — authoritative online world pickups

The frozen public M7 option enables authored additional world items. It does not
grant a spawn loadout. The authority creates one scene-owned pickup layout and
ledger before binding any owner, using the exact frozen match start, map content
identity, manifest and registry. The27 stock layouts retain their existing authored
centres, spawn1,1800-tick respawn and16-round reserve refill. Native items are neither
replaced nor assigned a custom weapon ID.

Every public life starts empty. Genuine accepted native input opens its journal
and source history; it does not grant a rifle. The actual player item phase checks
the current living actor, native pickup permission and contact geometry against
the available item incarnation. This follows that world's custom action processing
and native selection release. A successful first contact funds4/16 at source+1,
leaving the already released source native. Repeat contacts add capped reserve
without resetting magazine, cadence, reload, optic, selection or action sequence.

Timed powerups and ordinary weapons have distinct permission rules. The existing
`ModPickupContactPose` retains the native DisablePowerups restriction for timed
claims. `ModWeaponPickupContactPose` shares its geometry, liveness, camera and
IgnoreItemPickups gates while allowing weapon collection when timed powerups are
disabled, as ordinary native weapons already do. Both offline and online custom
weapon contact use this weapon-specific pose.

Each owner life retains a private pickup-knowledge fence. Genuine current ingress
records original source knowledge and ingress world before engine consumption;
the first binding uses the original boundary evidence before any contact can
fund it. A successful verified ledger outcome rotates the owner's unpredictable
128-bit nonce and resource revision. Private state publishes that stamp, actual
grant world and effective source alongside ownership. These fields participate
in state change detection even when a capped refill leaves ammo unchanged.

Input and control keep their creation-time stamps from05s. The first matching
input/control echo freezes the preceding accepted source frontier. Canonical
action admission additionally requires the exact current connection, bootstrap,
owner life, manifest and weapon; matching original source knowledge; a source
strictly after the echo frontier and at/after effective funding; and original
ingress world at/after the grant. Fire ACKs must also be at/after the grant and
bootstrap worlds. Missing/old knowledge rejects terminally through ordinary
source admission. Canonical age, pose, aim, resource, geometry and payment checks
remain mandatory. Already accepted emissions and terminal result identities are
not relabelled when knowledge changes or the shooter respawns.

Live protocol74 adds public item-state97 and application-ACK98. The134-byte state
contains the recipient's exact bootstrap/manifest, layout digest, bounded item
index/count, authority world, publication serial, spawn/incarnation, ready world,
availability and ledger revision. The138-byte ACK repeats the recipient/world,
index/serial and exact SHA256 payload digest. Neither message contains owner
resources or private knowledge. Native enums, packets and archive bodies remain
unchanged; historical73 remains readable and movement-v3 still starts at71.

Each recipient mailbox retains one immutable outstanding fact per authored item,
bounded by64 items. An unsent, wrong-scope, wrong-serial or wrong-digest ACK cannot
release it. Exact retries reuse their original bytes. Every recipient must have
acknowledged its baseline, and publication/serial capacity must exist before
contact funding. The single authority thread publishes the resulting consumed
incarnation into those reserved cells before releasing the ledger outcome. An
unexpected trusted failure quarantines the world rather than refunding or
reoffering a potentially funded incarnation. ACK timeouts use the existing
bounded custom-stream disconnection policy.

The authority advances respawn by simulation ticks and explicitly publishes
availability. Clients never infer permission to collect from a local timer or
overlap. Receivers hide items until their complete bounded baseline exists and
reject stale serials, contradictory duplicates, backwards incarnations/revisions,
and changed deadlines within an incarnation. A late recipient receives the
current consumed or available incarnation. Ordinary Hello/bootstrap retries keep
their original identity and inventory. Actual removal/rejoin advances generation
and starts empty. An invented same-life replacement is not an implicit regrant or
source-admission escape; a future native transition of that kind would require
explicit obligation-preserving rebind support.

Online drawing reads only the synchronized receiver and shares the original
procedural fallback with offline pickups. It cannot initialize a registry or
mutate delivery state from a render pass. First acquisition cues and eligible
automatic selection are consumed once at a fresh local input sample; explicit
selection, shooting, blocked input or native weapon priority can suppress that
one attempt. Subsequent selection uses the acquired-only wheel/cycling path.
Refills cue without forcing another equip. Final rifle assets remain CW-06.

Validation results, declared fixture seams and remaining release gates are kept
in the separate evidence record. Custom replay remains blocked until CW-12.
