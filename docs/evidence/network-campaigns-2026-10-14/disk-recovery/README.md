# Lossless capacity recovery

The long8 wrapper hit its unchanged 1.5 GiB guard while final reports were written. Its failure and partial receipt remain unchanged. No current campaign, runtime, asset or recording was removed. Inactive historical native-state JSON was stored as gzip after a full decoded SHA256/byte equality check. Every original byte remains restorable.

The manifest records 146 exact original/archive identities, decoded checks and restoration instructions. Free space increased from the observed 1.092 GiB to 8.008 GiB. This is storage recovery, not a simulation or campaign acceptance result. Archives and original native-state contents remain external.
