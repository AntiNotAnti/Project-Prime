## Accepted evidence and its limits

Five valid, matched 12-second unprofiled samples per arm used an interleaved fixed order, one physical candidate harness and binary, eight players in MP3 PROVING GROUND, Original preset, High shadows, 1920×1080, uncapped rendering and VSync off on Apple M4 Pro/macOS. The first OFF sample overlapped a build during warmup and was conservatively excluded and repeated. All retained samples report zero dropped ticks/stalls and approximately 60 Hz simulation. The figures below are medians of each run's frame percentiles, not percentiles pooled across runs.

| Frame metric | OFF (ms) | ON (ms) | Change |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| p50 | 2.2099 | 2.1414 | -3.10% |
| p95 | 4.1257 | 4.3387 | +5.16% |
| p99 | 4.8943 | 5.4449 | +11.25% |

Two separate profiled arms retained 27 completed windows each, after the simulation measurement-start marker and four discarded completed post-marker windows. Median window averages show source floats scaled per frame falling from 7107.13 to 1804.20; ON has 37.97 hits and 13.82 misses per frame and still copies 8921.67 floats into independent item snapshots. Median window p95 palette collection time falls from 0.012205 to 0.006998 ms, an absolute 0.005207 ms. These are descriptive per-window aggregates of inclusive CPU wall scopes; they do not isolate driver time or establish a total-frame benefit. Native GPU timing is **UNVERIFIED**.

Exact same-binary OFF/ON fidelity passes all 29 comparisons: 18 decoded RGBA world/HUD images and 11 float32 world/repeat/shadow depth files. Captures cover eight-player High shadows at 1920×1080 and solo Kanden with shadows Off at 1280×720, simulation frames 240/540/840/1140. All four capture contexts loaded the accepted candidate MVID. Recorded owner/name retirement and current-program-zero checks pass after those scenes unload; this is not a driver-residency or full resource-stress proof.

The plan's approximate 10% owner-work or 3–5% total tail improvement targets require no material degradation elsewhere. The source arithmetic reduction does not override the observed p95/p99 deterioration. Synthetic palette microbenchmarks remain eligibility evidence only. Do not enable this candidate by default or claim a rollout performance win.
