# Dedicated mantle and grapple animation

Mantle and grapple previously reused native jump/fall poses; grapple also drew its rope. Both now have procedural skeletal animation on all seven native Hunter rigs. Mantle adds a free-arm reach, torso hoist and knee lift. Grapple points the free arm toward the accepted anchor, adds a hanging lean, and bends and swings the legs. First person adds a small cannon dip and recovery while native firing, charge and switching clips keep playing. No imported animation clips or extracted game assets are published.

The pose exists only during render submission. Every native node and palette is restored afterward, including exception paths. The shooting arm keeps its native matrices. Camera aim, canonical muzzle, collision, native animation cursors, movement and accepted controllers do not consume the cosmetic pose. Bone-attached armor and weighted replacement skins share the body pose; unsupported skeletons retain their native animation. Forms, abilities and lifecycle cancellation keep priority.

Accepted mantle progress is published for remote players and passive replay. Grapple uses the existing accepted anchor. [Protocol 84](../network/parkour-animation-version-contract.md) carries this presentation; Parkour gameplay remains v5 and Classic remains the default.
