# Dedicated mantle and grapple animation

Mantle and grapple previously retained ordinary native jump/fall poses; grapple additionally drew a rope. They now have procedural body animation on all seven native Hunter rigs: free-arm reach, torso hoist and knee lift for mantle; anchor-directed free-arm reach, hanging lean, bent knees and leg swing for grapple. First person adds a restrained cannon dip/recovery while native firing/charge clips remain active. These are code-authored skeletal poses; no game assets or third-party animation clips are published.

The body pose is applied only during render submission and restored exactly afterward. The shooting arm stays in its native pose. Simulation, collision, movement, weapon aim, native animation cursors and camera do not consume the cosmetic pose. Accepted mantle phase travels through authority snapshots for remote players and passive replay; grapple uses its existing accepted anchor. See the [protocol84 contract](../network/parkour-animation-version-contract.md). Parkour gameplay stays v5, and Classic remains default.

Qualification is in progress. Development CPU checks pass actual keyboard-earned mantle/grapple for seven Hunters and both LODs, including weighted palette displacement, right-arm preservation, exact scope restoration, repeated-draw isolation, native-state guards and warmed allocation checks. This development pass is not a frozen producer or GL acceptance claim. Final committed-source results, images, exact hashes, compatibility checks and remaining gaps will be recorded before publication.
