## Top combat judgment and architecture cross-check

**Judgment remains Critical, source-confirmed:** the normal admitted claim path is enabled and gated to InMatch/MatchReady (`DedicatedServer.cs:1261–1271`; `NetHitClaims.cs:76`). Once those real safeguards hold, `Judge` checks historical victim proximity and plausibility ceilings but ordinary claims do not consult an authorized accepted fire/attack ledger. The only explicit launch-history lookup is the chamber Imperialist exception (`NetHitClaims.cs:1403–1404`). Tick's independently resolved hit ledger prevents duplicate payment; absence of a hit enables rescue rather than rejecting an unauthorized attack. ApplyOne supplies no omitted ordinary attack-authorization check. This conclusion does not depend on reviving prior client-authority architecture or bypassing phase/admission checks.

**Recommended scope:** add bounded attack authorization and shot-specific legal outcomes using the existing match/epoch/slot/generation/life/ShotId identity and source/ACK timing. Accepted fire/alt-contact evidence can still authorize a legitimate local hit whose physical authority collision misses. An independently resolved authority hit must not be required for every rescue; that removes the documented purpose of claims. Retain grace arbitration, traveling shots, direct+splash, ricochet and headshot-difference behavior. Validate against the world the shooter acknowledged and existing dynamic history, not defender-aware rewind or movement replay.

The relevant invariants explicitly require shooter-acknowledged rewind, local outgoing prediction, late rescue and explicit verdicts (`ARCHITECTURE-INVARIANTS.md:67–74`), owner-reported movement/no same-life reconciliation (`:44–61`), and ShotId+life identity independent of timing (`:243–247`). The proposal must preserve all of them. Resource validation does not imply changing that movement model. Packet layout changes, if actually needed, require an explicit protocol bump; a server-side policy ledger alone should not trigger an unsolicited wire redesign.

**Immediate mitigation is optional, not a silent feature-loss default:** temporarily disabling unsupported claim rescue can be exposed as an explicit operator integrity setting while the attack-validation work is pending. Its latency/packet-loss/kill-confirmation tradeoff must be communicated and measured. Do not assert that disabling all claims by default is a behavior-preserving fix. Before changing the production default, test RTT0/80/150/320/750ms with jitter/loss/reordering, short/long flights, body/head/quickscope, boost/turret, direct+splash and mutual kills. Separate security-policy rejection from missed legitimate evidence, compare rescued/refused rates and authoritative/local outcome agreement, and retain explicit verdict diagnostics.
