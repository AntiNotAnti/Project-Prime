## Final Windows timing gate declared before the final matrix

Use one frozen build, 8 bots, MP3 PROVING GROUND, 1280x720, Original, shadows Off,
100% scale and four seconds of warmup. No build or test workloads may run during
the final timing matrix/soak. Preserve every failure and raw interval.

For numeric caps, the diagnostic gate is mean completed callback FPS within
2% of the selected cap, p99 interval at most twice its target period, zero
simulation drops/stalls, and complete chronological storage. Display/Unlimited
have functional/identity gates rather than a guessed hardware scanout rate.
The 30-minute run independently requires zero unexplained drops/stalls and
complete storage. These are application-cadence gates, not photon/VRR/network
or universal performance acceptance. Capture overhead itself remains an
independent unqualified cost; no total FPS gain is inferred.
