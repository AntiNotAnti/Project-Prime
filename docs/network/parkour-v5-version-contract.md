# Parkour v5 version contract

Protocol 81 selects optional Parkour v5. Protocol 80 retains v4, 79 retains v3, 78 retains v2 and 77 retains v1. Classic remains the default; model 4 and modifier bit 19 are unchanged. Live peers require the exact protocol. V5 inherits V4 horizontal/stance tuning.

Keyboard G is dedicated Grapple in gameplay. Pad action 37 is appended and unbound; touch action 24 is appended and hidden by default, including old layouts without an explicit saved action. Existing IDs and replay G easing context are preserved. Each physical fixed-tick press advances a nonzero ushort request nonce, wrapping 65535 to 1. The nonce remains on release. A cancelled or denied hold cannot retry until a new press.

Offline/native-authority owners choose a nearest safe static anchor from a body-relative eye origin after native aim update. Online authority independently chooses from jointly accepted current-life position and aim on the scene thread. No client anchor exists. Match, authority epoch, generation, life, source-frame and nonce ordering fence admission. Generic recovered combat presses cannot grant a grapple. Same-nonce release or Jump publishes an inactive tombstone; newer release before press consumes that request. Rejected poses do not refresh accepted-input freshness.

Anchor distance is 2 to 24 world units. Unsafe first polygons, entity collision, closed doors/active fields and other body obstructions refuse admission. Native nearest polygon queries retain their candidate lease through consumption; more than 8,192 candidates, 4,096 face visits or 256 actor checks fail closed. Actor envelopes conservatively refuse near doors/fields/players/turrets; enemies use native hurt volumes. These bounds intentionally sacrifice some attachments near complex geometry.

An accepted hold lasts at most 180 fixed 60 Hz ticks. Online human owner forces wait for the matching accepted lifecycle and nonce; physical release/cancellation suppresses forces immediately. Authority expires after 30 ticks without a jointly accepted current request. Owner loses force permission after 30 network frames without an actual snapshot. Gravity, native jump/abilities, forms, pads, teleports, collision and external acceleration keep priority. A unilateral velocity constraint removes outward radial velocity only when taut and adds at most 12/60 = 0.2 units/second inward assistance per tick. Tangential and incoming inward velocity remain intact. No position projection, reconciliation or trajectory resimulation is introduced. Human movement stays owner-reported; bots remain authority-owned; shadow validation stays advisory.
