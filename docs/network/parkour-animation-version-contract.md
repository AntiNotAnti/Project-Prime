# Protocol84: Parkour animation presentation

Protocol84 adds mantle presentation without retuning movement. Parkour remains v5; Classic remains default. Protocol82/83 retain the original v5 movement and1298-byte canonical native-touch intent. Protocol83's exact hosting/archive selection and detached custom resource contracts remain available. No packet ID, match bit, movement coefficient or owner-reported human trajectory rule changes.

The new `MantleVisualPhase` is0 for inactive and1–15 for normalized climb progress. An owner computes it from the admitted mantle controller's actual lift/across/settle progress. Its one-byte hint is appended after the native touch report and ushort grapple nonce: canonical intent1299, old touch end1296, nonce at1296–1297, phase at1298. Compact intents append the same byte after the nonce; header/native-touch/fire offsets stay unchanged. Maximum14-fire relayed SlotIntent is exactly1200bytes including header24 and slot1; native fallback remains available for custom wrappers. Unknown phase values16–255 are refused before codec publication.

Authority consumes the hint only at the existing jointly accepted current-life pose/control/stance seam. A hint never runs mantle collision queries, grants forces or changes gameplay eligibility. Repeated/older source frames and rejected poses cannot refresh the visual lease. Authority clears publication after30 scene ticks without accepted input. The accepted phase occupies the previously unused upper nibble of the PlayerState auxiliary byte; the lower nibble's turret, protection, crouch and tuck meanings remain unchanged. Canonical192 and fast138 player widths, slow77 entry and full fast1172-byte datagram are unchanged. Remote presentation consumes the accepted snapshot nibble, with a live30-tick actual-snapshot source lease, rather than raw relayed hints. Local owner animation follows its own admitted controller and immediate cancellation.

Native checkpoint16 declares feature64 (`ParkourPose`) alongside native-touch/pickups/grants/grapple:87 without timed shots,95 with them. Custom checkpoint17 retains custom feature32, yielding119/127. Native14 for82/83 and custom15 for83 remain frozen at192/1298 actor rows; new16/17 rows use192/1299. Published12/13 widths173/1296 are unchanged. Old packet adapters and direct checkpoint readers clear the reserved phase nibble and default the new hint byte to0. No historical jump becomes a climb.

World11/native and12/custom payload layouts remain unchanged. Both embedded decoders carry the new declared phase, and passive world restoration installs it from the canonical player state after graph restoration. Passive playback retains the last recorded accepted phase until another recorded snapshot changes it; it does not age that fact using a live network timeout. No local controls, controller timers, collision references or forces are restored. The explicit legacy graph schema/fingerprint stays unchanged. Native/custom historical construction protocol must agree with archive metadata.

The body animation is an independently implemented procedural layer on the supplied native Hunter skeletons, not an imported binary clip. It reaches with the free arm, leans/hoists the torso and lifts the knees through the accepted climb. Grapple uses its existing accepted anchor to aim the free arm, lean into the rope and tuck/swing the legs. Native shooting-arm matrices and animation cursors stay intact. A scoped palette loan surrounds body submission and restores every native node and matrix palette before muzzle, effects or another actor can consume them. Both native LODs and weighted replacement skins follow the same posed palette. Unsupported skeletons keep their native animation.

First-person presentation adds a small cannon dip/recovery to the prepared render gun transform. It leaves camera orientation/position, aim, canonical cannon nodes, firing and collision unchanged; presentation muzzle effects use the same prepared transform. The native gun clips continue to own charging, firing and switching. Forms, abilities, damage locks, death, teleport and cancellation retain priority. No new camera shake or input binding is introduced.

Qualification is separately attributed in [animation results](../movement/parkour-animation-results.md). A visual change does not establish physical-device, achieved-refresh, Android or performance acceptance. Roll back optional gameplay with Classic; a code rollback must preserve protocol84 and published checkpoint readers or explicitly refuse them.
