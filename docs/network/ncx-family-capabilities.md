# NCX-05 native family capabilities and fallbacks

`CombatFamilyPolicies` records independent proof obligations from existing
`WeaponLagPolicies` and native multiplayer mechanics. It centralizes the two
already-qualified receipt families and the existing Imperialist native Shadow
selector. It promotes zero fresh-hit families. Existing native, pending, continuous,
self/world/bot and custom-weapon handlers retain their behavior.

The table distinguishes three scopes:

| Scope | Current support | Meaning |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Exact already-paid primary direct Power Beam/Imperialist | Eligible receipt | NCX-04's existing full-key, weapon/head, admission and terminal-cache gates; no new payment |
| Native-ordered first-segment Imperialist | Shadow | Existing admitted/funded source, actual segment/witness and pre-damage legal gates; no claim enforcement |
| Fresh native-family claim | Shadow fallback | Missing independent proof obligations; metadata classification is not a completed family evaluator |
| Unknown mechanics or custom live networking | Unsupported | Existing denial/native fallback; no custom-to-native identity alias |

A primary family label uses the existing source-capability precedence. Independent
overlays retain additional obligations; a homing child with ricochet and effects
cannot lose those requirements when its primary label becomes Child. Source,
funding, exact source and victim lifecycles, historical actors, dynamic world,
native point/head, legal damage/effects and closed ordering remain required.
`WeaponLagPolicy.UsesHistoricalDynamicGeometry=false` never becomes a dynamic LOS
certificate merely because a timing mode says HistoricalTrace.

| Mechanics/context | Additional obligations before any fresh promotion |
| --- | --- |
| Direct/straight | Native complete ray/competitor/world order, exact impact/head/life and legal channel |
| Projectile, gravity or speed evolution | Full swept flight and collision clock; endpoint distance is insufficient |
| Homing projectile | Fire-time target lifecycle and lawful curve history, independently of the primary timing label |
| Ricochet or derived/multiple components | Bounce path/surface and/or exact component ancestry; child owner/entity birth and retirement |
| Splash and AOE | Radius/center/occlusion; splash is independent of AOE flags |
| Freeze, burn or disrupt | Exact effect frame, susceptibility, source-life funding and native effect facts |
| Continuous tracking | Existing pulse/target phase, target lifecycle and capped healing; no projectile-curve shortcut |
| Turret, bombs and alt/melee | Owner plus spawned identity, birth/retirement, morph/lifecycle and component/kind |
| Future custom definitions | Immutable definition/version/manifest plus authenticated live adapter; dormant codec validity is not authentication |

The generic receipt column does not replace the existing dedicated continuous
paid-tick branch. Those handlers remain active before the generic Ready seam.
Homing, splash or multiple-emission metadata also cannot invalidate an already
recorded primary direct payment: the receipt's exact actual component/kind and
material outcome remain authoritative. Unsupported request shapes stay legacy.

`From(EquipInfo)` freezes nominal mechanics without retaining mutable equipment.
Native Spawn selects charged flags, afflictions, decay and ricochet arrays at the
partial-charge minimum, while its amount helper still uses uncharged values there.
The next charge step can activate homing. Nonchargeable weapons retain uncharged
amounts even when dormant charged metadata is nonzero. The existing timing resolver
is reused, including its exact-minimum homing correction.

`CombatFamilies` is a bounded report-time metadata inventory. It describes nominal
normal/affinity rows and charge samples; Balanced, actor, derived-entity and actual
emission overrides still need their actual native facts. Requirements are obligations,
not satisfied evidence. Reporting neither authenticates a source nor creates a payment,
geometry witness, grant, target selection or closed causal frontier.

Custom M7 remains an offline prototype. Its immutable registry/digest and distinct
custom action identity do not register a live sender/receiver or convert into native
ShotKey. Existing online custom damage suppression and native-witness/payment fences
remain in place. No new custom adapter or protocol layout is introduced here.
