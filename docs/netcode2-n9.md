# N9: canonical player forms and detached turrets

N9 carries canonical morph phase, transition age, paired body/turret health, turret presence and detached anchor through the slow lane. Protocol 49 uses 173-byte canonical player state, 28-byte form state, 58-byte slow player rows and unchanged 138-byte fast rows. The focused eight-player SnapshotFast datagram fixture reaches 1172 bytes.

Observers reconcile the authority transition age and preserve detached turret anchors while the mobile upper body moves. The immediate owner retains predicted morph/unmorph responsiveness; its animation can lead the canonical observer phase. Form health reconciliation respects newer combat acknowledgements, confirmed death, generation/life fences and turret destruction. Remorph creates a fresh turret episode without allowing an older episode to resurrect or remove it.

Authority turret firing retains native target, cooldown and form gates. Predictive observer playback reproduces accepted authored turret events with their exact ownership keys and authored pose independently of the current form animation. Observer reproduction does not create authority damage. An authority-retired source event is counted by its exact full key, with an explicit terminal disposition; it is not silently treated as a fired authority projectile.

Accepted semantic baselines now contribute to the known event/award frontiers even when the late observer never received the individual historical award. Raw receive deduplication and announcement history remain separate. This prevents a baseline-only award from producing a synthetic shutdown-drain gap.
